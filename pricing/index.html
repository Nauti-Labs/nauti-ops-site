Pricing for a desk
that already has too many tabs.

A paid 90-day Founding Pilot, a Desk plan, a per-agency monthly plan, and Enterprise when the book is bigger than one office.

Plans

One agency means one company on one Triton workspace. A vessel call is a port call opened during the month. Pilot is prepaid for 90 days. Desk and Agency are month to month. Booking confirms the rate before anything is billed.

Questions desks actually ask

Who are these plans for?
Small and mid ship agencies and operators running calls on the Houston Ship Channel. Desk is one working board. Agency is the whole office. Enterprise is more than one office, or coverage past this channel.
Is the Founding Pilot free?
No. It is a paid 90-day engagement so both sides treat it like a live book, not a tire kick. The fee and the call cap are on the Pilot card.
What is in the console?
Voyages, vessels, cargos, Statement of Facts, the berth and terminal board, customer updates, and Captain AI. The sample console on this site is demo data.
Can we move from Pilot to Agency?
Yes. Book Agency when the 90 days end, or sooner if the desk is already living in it. Enterprise is a conversation, not a checkout.
